Source code of Duel Synapse Arena

The Arena runs the EDOPro rules engine and the Project Ignis card scripts, which are licensed under the GNU Affero General Public License v3.0 or later. As required by that licence, the complete source of the program you are using is available here, free of charge.

Rules engine

  • edo9300/ygopro-core at commit baa3881d0bae48e9994f915f5c931be53c1ba8ef (version 11.0, unmodified), with Lua at 6e22fedb74cf0c9b6656e9fce8b7331db847c605.
  • WebAssembly bridge: kx_shim.cpp
  • Build scripts: build.sh (engine) and build-runtimes.sh (C++ runtimes with exceptions, from llvm-project llvmorg-21.1.4, compiled with wasi-sdk 30).
  • Compiled engine: ocgcore.wasm

Card scripts and data

  • ProjectIgnis/CardScripts, unmodified, at the commit listed in scripts.json (commit field).
  • Card data from ProjectIgnis/BabelCDB and strings from ProjectIgnis/Distribution: cards.json, strings.json.

Arena client

These files are served exactly as written, without minification.

  • index.html, arena.css, app.js, deck.js, net.js, host-worker.js
  • engine/core.js, engine/host.js, engine/data.js, engine/rules.js, engine/bot.js, engine/constants.js
  • How it fits together, and how to rebuild it: README.md

The message layouts in engine/core.js follow n1xx1/ocgcore-wasm (MIT). MEIOCG is not affiliated with or endorsed by Konami.
