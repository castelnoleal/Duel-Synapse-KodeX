Get listed

Open KodeX → Settings → Profile → Edit profile and turn on Show me on MEIOCG Players.
Points come from using KodeX: each day you open it (10), each duel in Duel Synapse Arena (4), each deck you build (5) and the cards you look up (1 each, up to 300).
Only what your profile shows is listed: name, title, about line, preset icon, frame and activity counts. No email or account. Turn it off and you're removed.
